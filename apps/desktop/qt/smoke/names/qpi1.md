# Object names: QPI1 (wave 1 integration)

No section of its own: the checks are in the sections of the screens they join,
named `qpi1: ...` (`qp0-components`, `qp1-sidebar`, `qp2-channel`,
`qp10-settings`, `qp13-devices`).

| Name | What |
|---|---|
| `RightPane.page` | the Loader under the graph holding the selected channel's page: `InputDetail` (`input`) or `OutputDetail` (`output`), from QP2 |
| `DeviceStatus.button` | (QP1's) `notice` is `LinkController.lockNotice`; `tip` is `LinkController.sessionTip` during a hub session |
