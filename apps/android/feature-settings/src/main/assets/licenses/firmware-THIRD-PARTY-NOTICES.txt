DSPi firmware images: third-party notices

DSPi-RP2350-v1.1.6.uf2) are Weeb Labs' release v1.1.6, unmodified:
https://github.com/WeebLabs/DSPi/releases/tag/v1.1.6 (commit

https://github.com/Bofr5555/DSPi). The firmware is licensed under the GNU GPL
version 3; its complete source is that tag, with the pico-sdk submodule at
