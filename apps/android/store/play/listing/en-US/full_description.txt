REQUIRES A DSPi DEVICE. This app is a controller for the DSPi USB audio DSP (a Raspberry Pi Pico or Pico 2 running the DSPi firmware by Weeb Labs). It does not process or play audio on its own; without DSPi hardware you can explore it in Demo mode.

It is the Android client of uConsole for DSPi, made by Bofr5555 and published with Weeb Labs' permission. It needs DSPi firmware v1.1.6 for full features; with other versions it connects read-only.

Set up your speakers in the app: say which output drives a woofer, a mid or a tweeter, and a tweeter keeps its protection. Measuring and auto-tune with a microphone are in the desktop and web consoles of uConsole for DSPi.

Optional DSPi Link (off by default): control DSPi units over your local network through a hub. Links to a uConsole for DSPi hub are encrypted, and pairing pins the hub's key. Links to Weeb Labs' DSPi Console or to a DSPi bridge are not encrypted; use those only on networks you trust.
